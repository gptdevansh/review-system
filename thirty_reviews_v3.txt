=== REVIEW 01 ===
We four elderly friends from Kolkata had been talking about Nainital for probably ten years. Finally booked through Gola Holidays this March and honestly the whole thing went smoother than expected. Driver on the Kathgodam stretch was patient, never rushed on the ghats which our old backs appreciated. Hotel was five minutes from Mall Road so evening strolls by the lake were easy. One afternoon we just sat at Tiffin Top for hours talking like old college days, forgot what time it was. Morning tea in the room tasted better than it had any right to. Four days of actual rest.

=== REVIEW 02 ===
Sitabani safari booking Gola Holidays se karwaya tha. Tiger nahi dikha, honestly expect bhi nahi kiya tha, par jungle ka mahaul hi alag hota hai. Jeep gate par time se tha, koi wait nahi. Raste mein teen char peacocks bhi dikh gaye. Peaceful trip raha, bas itna kaafi tha.

=== REVIEW 03 ===
Station se tempo traveller time pe aa gayi thi Ramnagar mein, gaadi bhi clean thi jo expecting nahi tha itna. Papa ko back ka problem hai toh seat dekh ke thoda chinta tha, par puri drive mein unhe koi takleef nahi hui. Jungle ke saath wala rasta jab dhoop pedo ke beech se aati thi us waqt mumma ne bola puri zindagi yahi photo dikhaunga. Chhoti chhoti cheezein yaad rehti hain.

=== REVIEW 04 ===
Room se directly Naini Lake dikhti thi aur Mall Road paidal paanch minute tha. Nainital mein isse zyada kya chahiye honestly.

=== REVIEW 05 ===
Jeep teen char minute late thi Sitabani ke liye, jungle roads mein toh yahi hota hai. Par andar pohanchte hi bacchon ko iski koi parwaah nahi rahi, pehle hi modd pe teen peacocks dikhe track ke bilkul paas. Wo moment kaafi tha baaki sab bhulane ke liye.

=== REVIEW 06 ===
Char Dham yatra mein do bachhe saath the toh drive ke baare mein thodi tension thi, Yamunotri se Gangotri wali stretch kaafi lambi hai. Par Gola Holidays ne jo gaadi di thi wo spacious thi, bacche beech beech mein soo bhi gaye. Har halting hotel mein garam paani ready tha aur bistar acha tha, jo itni lambi drive ke baad bahut matter karta hai. Raat ko sona bilkul possible ho gaya, jo uss trah ke safar mein hona chahiye.

=== REVIEW 07 ===
Sabah khidki kholi Auli wale hotel mein aur seedha Nanda Devi peak saamne. Gola Holidays ne booking ki thi. Dosto ne pehle dekha toh ekdum chup ho gaye, phir shor shuru hua. Aisi view toh picture mein bhi nahi hoti.

=== REVIEW 08 ===
Nanda Devi peak directly bahar se dikhna tha Auli mein, wo toh tha hi. Hotel buffet average tha, khaas nahi tha, par view ke saamne koi shikayat nahi karta.

=== REVIEW 09 ===
Jim Corbett Museum Choti Haldwani mein tha, driver wahan le gaya trip ke beech mein, hum log expect nahi kiye the itna kuch hoga andar. Corbett ke apne kapde, purani tasveerein diwaaron par, jo ped unhone khud lagaye the wahan aaj bhi hain. Subah ka waqt tha toh bheed nahi thi, aaram se ghoom sake. Un bade bade aam ke pedo ke neeche kuch der khade rahe, wahaan bahut hi sukoon wala mahaul tha. Poori trip ka sabse quiet moment wahi raha.

=== REVIEW 10 ===
Maa do ghante window ke paas baithke Naini Lake ki taraf dekhti rahi, dhund lift hoti dekh ke. Hum Gola Holidays se book karke aaye the aur hotel Mall Road se paidal thi isliye parking ya steep climb ka jhanjhat nahi pada. Floorring wooden thi, kamre mein sham ke baad thandi nahi lagti thi. Pahad par aisi subah milna mushkil hota hai city mein.

=== REVIEW 11 ===
Pehli baar Dhikala ke liye subah ka canter liya Ramnagar se. Hum log bahut saalon baad India mein the toh ye experience aur bhi alag laga. Ramganga valley ke upar se jab dhund uthti thi, darshani doori tak ghaas ke maidan dikhte the. Aur tab jungle ke andar se ek bada haathi raste par nikla. Uski taraf dekhte reh gaye bas.

=== REVIEW 12 ===
Bacche Gun Hill ki cable car ke baare mein ab bhi baat karte hain ghar mein. Socha tha Company Garden ke chakkar mein aur Kempty Falls ki thand mein ek do baar rona dhona toh hoga. Nahi hua. Pura rasta wo khidki se chipke rahe pahad ka rang badalta dekh ke. Driver ko pata tha kahan chai milti hai, chhoti wali crampy ho jaati thi hairpin pe toh wahan ruk jaate the. Kamra itna bada tha ki bacche ghoom lete the sightseeing ke baad bhi. Ab ghar waapis aa gaye Delhi mein, seedha flat feel hoti hai pahad ke baad.

=== REVIEW 13 ===
Subah jeep resort gate pe time se thi, suraj abhi pura nikla bhi nahi tha. Hum chaar dost cram ho gaye peeche. Boundary ke paas ek stretch thi jo kaafi rough thi, pothole mein sab uchhal rahe the koi bees minute tak. Par jaise Bijrani zone ke andar gaye atmosphere hi badal gaya. Sal ke ped itne ghane the ki subah ki dhund unhee mein atak gayi thi. Waterhole ke paas chup baithe rahe, guide pugmarks dikha raha tha zameen pe. Phir tiger tall grass se seedha raste par aa gaya, pachaas foot se zyada door nahi tha, aur wapas ghane jungle mein chala gaya. Kuch nahi bola us waqt koi.

=== REVIEW 14 ===
Mere naati Auli mein pehli baar barfh dekh rahe the. Gola Holidays ka trip tha. Nanda Devi ropeway wali height se dikhti hai, unki aankhein dekhi toh mujhe bhi aansu aaye. Teen raat warm kamre mein aaram se soye. Har jagah pe gaadi ready rehti thi, humein kahi intezaar nahi karna pada. Budhape mein bacchon ke saath phaad ghoomna possible hua iska kaaaran yahi tha.

=== REVIEW 15 ===
Kathgodam se Munsiyari Innova mein chaudah ghante ka rasta hai. Thakaan toh aayi, jhooth nahi bolunga. Par jab ek ek hairpin pe snow peaks nazar aane lage toh thakaan ka dhyan hi nahi raha.

=== REVIEW 16 ===
Ek hi din mein Nainital, Bhimtal, Sattal, Naukuchiatal sab ghoom aaye Ramnagar se nikal ke. Innova bilkul clean thi, pahadi raste pe comfortable raha safar. Sattal mein jab saat jheel ek saath nazar aayi dopahar ki roshni mein, camera nikalna bhi bhool gaya thodi der. Vehicle ne steep roads pe koi problem nahi ki.

=== REVIEW 17 ===
Corbett Falls gaye the elders ke saath. Chhota sa rasta tha unke liye bhi aasaan raha. Tiger nahi mila safari mein, deer the, elephant ke panje mile zameen pe, par wahan ka sukoon hi alag hota hai city ke shor ke baad. Raat ko aankhein band hote hi neend aa gayi, subah uthke laga tha sach mein kahi aur aa gaye hain.

=== REVIEW 18 ===
Bhimtal aur Naukuchiatal couple ke liye bilkul sahi jagah hai. Subah naav chalai, paani saaf tha, koi halchal nahi thi ghat pe. Jungle safari mein khaas kuch nahi dikha, door se kuch hila tha bas, par jungle ka feel hi alag hota hai. Ghar wapas aa gaye lekin wahan ki thand aur paani ki awaaz abhi bhi yaad aati hai.

=== REVIEW 19 ===
Innova Crysta mein Kathgodam se Munsiyari chale. Rasta lamba aur ghumawdaar hai, papa ki sehat ki fikr thi, par gaadi ki ride ne thaka nahi. Driver uncle dheemai gati se chal rahe the, ghuma-phira ke bhi smooth laga safar. Hotel mein room milne mein thodi der lagi, par jaise bahar ki view dikhi sab theek laga. Pahad ki choti cross karte waqt bas ek dusre ko dekha, samajh aa gaya ki aana sahi tha.

=== REVIEW 20 ===
Kausani mein subah paanch baje bacchon ko uthaya sunrise ke liye. Sabko laga yeh kya pagalpan hai. Par jaise hi Himalayas pe pehli roshni padi, sona rang ho gaya puri choti pe. Chota wala camera le ke bhag gaya seedha, photos pe photos khichta raha. Gola Holidays ki taraf se hotel aur gaadi dono time pe ready thi. Driver ne raste mein chai ke liye bhi ruka, koi jaldi nahi ki. Bacchon ko pahadi hawa alag hi lagti hai.

=== REVIEW 21 ===
Gypsy li thi Dhikala zone ke liye Ramnagar se. Rasta kaafi dusty tha, khidki khuli thi toh hawa bhi aa rahi thi, bacche har deer pe chilla rahe the. Resort mein wifi nahi tha teen din, par kisi ne mobile nahi pakda jab ek baghini ko stream cross karte dekha watchtower ke paas. Sunset se thodi der pehle ek awaaz aayi jungle se. Woh roar suno toh ek baar toh sar khaada ho jaata hai.

=== REVIEW 22 ===
Nainital ke liye subah nikal gaye, raaste mein Bhimtal aur Sattal bhi dekha. Gaadi clean thi, kaafi comfortable rahi puri drive. Naukuchiatal pe baith ke paani dekhte rahe bina kisi rush ke, wahi moment worth tha sara safar.

=== REVIEW 23 ===
Ranikhet mein subah maa baap ke saath uthna aur chowk pe chai, bilkul alag hi hota hai. Hotel mein wifi practically nahi tha, par unhone bola theek hai beta, hum toh bas yahan ki hawa mein baithna chahte hain.

=== REVIEW 24 ===
Bijrani morning safari hamare Corbett trip ka sabse yaadgaar hissa raha. Gypsy paanch baje resort gate pe thi, permit ke liye andar wait nahi karna pada. Pehle ghante mein hi waterhole ke paas tiger dikha. Aur phir kuch bolna nahi tha kisi ko.

=== REVIEW 25 ===
Forest house Dhikala ka akele book karna mushkil hota hai, koi nahi karta. Gola Holidays ne sorted kar diya. Andar core zone mein neend khuli toh haathi ki aawaaz aa rahi thi jungle se. Canteen ka khana theek thak tha, par woh toh koi bhi bhool jaata hai aisi subah ke baad.

=== REVIEW 26 ===
Parda hataya subah mein Auli mein toh poora Nanda Devi range saamne tha. Maa ab bhi ghar pe iske baare mein baat karti hain. Ramnagar office se Gola Holidays ke sath baat ki thi, unhone ek wooden cottage suggest kiya tha jisme bade sheesha wali khidkiyan thi seedha peaks ki taraf. Baba ghanton balcony pe chai leke baithte the, pahad ka rang badalta dekhte. Khana sadha tha, ghar jaisa lagta tha, jo budhon ke liye ekdum sahi hota hai itni lambi drive ke baad. Ab Kolkata mein baith ke aankhein band karo toh wahi safed chotiyan dikhti hain.

=== REVIEW 27 ===
Tempo traveller mein jab bacche chadhe Ramnagar station pe toh pahli cheez yahi boli sab ne ki itni badi gaadi mili. Resort zyada door nahi tha, par jungle wala rasta khidki se dekh ke sab chilla rahe the pura rasta. Ghar wapas aaye mahine beet gaye, chota wala abhi bhi us gaadi aur jungle ke raste ki baat karta rehta hai.

=== REVIEW 28 ===
Munsiyari mein subah uthke balcony pe khade the chai ke saath jab achanak badal hata aur saare paanch Panchachuli peaks seedha saamne aa gaye. Kamre theek the, dining hall ka khana zyada special nahi tha. Par us subah lakdi ki kursiyon pe baith ke barfh ko sona hote dekhna, uss cheez ne sab cover kar diya.

=== REVIEW 29 ===
Saat saal ke bacche ko ghode pe Tiffin Top le jaana aaj pull off ho gaya, honestly expect nahi kiya tha. Pony wale log bahut helpful the, bacche ko thaame rahe aur pace slow rakhi. Hum paidal saath saath pine trees ke beech chale. Dorothy's Seat pohanche toh clouds uth chuke the, poori valley aur neecha dikhti Naini Lake bilkul map jaisi. Wahaan ek ghante baithe chai piya, bacche snow peaks dhundhne ki koshish karte rahe dhund mein. Gola Holidays ne subah hotel gate se cab di thi aur driver ne lower parking pe puri dopahar wait kiya bina kuch kahi.

=== REVIEW 30 ===
November mein Munsiyari mein jab subah uthke snow peaks seedhe cottage ki khidki ke bahar nazar aayi, kuch aur chahiye nahi tha. Bookings aur permits sab Gola Holidays ne sambhaal liye toh hum log bas pahad mein the.
